Welcome

A Refined Stay In The Heart Of Tzaneen

The Legacy Hotel & Spa is a hotel built around a simple idea: that a hotel should feel like somewhere you want to linger. Generous rooms, crisp linen, a restaurant and bar on site, and a spa designed for slowing down — all held together by a team that genuinely looks after the people who walk through the door.

Whether you are here for a night of business or a long weekend away, every space has been thought through, from the finishes in the suites to the quiet of the treatment rooms.
